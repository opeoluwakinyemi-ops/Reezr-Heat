Your privacy

Reezr Heat is run by Reezr Climate. You can use it without giving your name, phone number or email address.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your exact location never leaves your phone. We round it to about 1 km first.
  • We do not store your location on our servers unless you turn on alerts.
  • We count how the app is used, with a random number instead of your identity.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app shows no adverts.
  • Turning off alerts deletes your saved places from our server.

Your location and searches

When you tap “Use my location”, your phone asks your permission first. We round your position to about 1 km and send it to our weather and map services to get the reading and the name of the place. What you type in the search box goes to our place search services.

Your last reading, your saved places and your choices, such as the theme and who the advice is for, are kept on your phone. Clearing your browser’s data for this site removes them.

Heat alerts

Alerts are off until you turn them on. When they are on, our server keeps the names and rounded locations of your saved places, the notification address your phone’s browser gives us, and a note of the last warning we sent for each place. We use these only to check the forecast and send your warnings.

The notification address does not include your name. When you turn alerts off, or if the address stops working, we delete this record.

What we count

To see whether the app is useful, and to report totals to partners and funders, we record simple events. Each one is linked to a random number made on your phone, not to you. It covers:

  • When the app is opened and roughly how long it is used
  • Which tabs are opened
  • Whether a reading came from your location, a search, a saved place, the map or a shared link
  • The heat level shown, but not the place
  • Whether the app was installed or shared, and how
  • Whether you are on a phone or a computer
  • The country and state, estimated from your network

We do not store your internet address or your coordinates with these events. We keep the counts for as long as they are useful for reporting, and we have not yet set a fixed deletion date.

Services we rely on

Like any website, these services can see your internet address when your device connects to them, and they follow their own privacy policies.

Open-MeteoWeather forecasts and place search. Receives rounded locations and search text.
BigDataCloudFinds the name of your place when you use your location. Receives your rounded location.
Photon (Komoot) and OpenStreetMap NominatimExtra place search. Receives search text.
CloudflareHosts the app and our database, and estimates the country and state for our counts.
Your browser’s notification serviceDelivers alerts if you turn them on. This may be Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device.
Google FontsSupplies the app’s lettering.

Your choices and rights

You can use search instead of your location, turn alerts off at any time, stop being counted using the button above, and clear the app’s data from your browser.

You can also ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Because we do not collect names, we may need your help to identify your record. Nigeria’s Data Protection Act gives you rights over your personal data, and you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes and contact

If we change how the app uses your information, we will update this page and its date. For anything about your privacy, email hello@reezrheat.com.
